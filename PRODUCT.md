# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Astro + TypeScript. Motion via CSS + WAAPI. No client framework and no WebGL on the first ship; the page is a static document so LCP can be HTML. Content stays in typed modules so copy can be swapped later without a redesign.

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, and creative directors who land on a personal site for 20–40 seconds, often on a laptop, sometimes on a phone between meetings. They are scanning for craft, personality, and proof that this person can ship, not reading a résumé line by line.

Secondary: peers and friends sharing the site for the vibe.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that makes someone memorable in one sitting. Success is: the visitor can name the person, feel a specific world, and find work, education/career, and skills without hunting. The site is an Experience surface first, with Persuade moments only where a recruiter needs a next action (contact, résumé, project).

## Positioning

Not another dark-SaaS developer card grid. The site is a fusion of three lived cultures: epic game UI (World of Warcraft), night-city futurism (cyberpunk as chrome, rain, and CRT, not purple neon), and rock energy (Latin American rock stages plus Catfish and the Bottlemen indie-gig grit). The mechanism is atmosphere as identity: you remember the person because the page feels like a venue, not a template.

## Operating Context

Visited in a browser. First view is the hero. Then a scroll or staged path through: featured work, career/education, skills, contact. Audio is optional and never autoplays. Reduced motion must still tell the story. Content for work, education, and skills is placeholder until the owner fills it.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single-page portfolio with clear regions for Work, Education & Career, and Skills.
- Work slots may be empty placeholders. Do not invent projects, metrics, or extra employers.
- Contact is LinkedIn and GitHub.
- Must stay usable on mobile. The extraordinary moment lives on desktop; mobile gets a committed, lighter translation, not a amputated demo.
- No autoplaying sound. No login. No CMS in this phase.

## Brand Commitments

Binding visual constraints volunteered by the owner (do not expand into a palette here):

- Mix World of Warcraft, cyberpunk, Latin American rock, and Catfish and the Bottlemen.
- Striking, unique, animated. 3D is allowed if it earns the first viewport.
- Avoid generic AI portfolio looks.

Voice: bilingual-capable later (Spanish + English). For this phase, interface chrome can be Spanish or English but must be consistent. Prefer Spanish if a choice is needed. No SaaS buzzwords. No fake testimonials.

## Product Principles

1. Atmosphere is identity. If you strip the copy, the page still belongs to this person.
2. Work, path, and skills are findable in seconds. Spectacle never hides the résumé job.
3. One extraordinary moment, not a carnival. The rest of the site supports it.
4. Placeholders are honest. Never dress empty slots as real case studies.
5. Accessible first, cinematic second. Reduced motion still communicates.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast. Visible keyboard focus. `prefers-reduced-motion` fallbacks for every non-essential animation. Touch targets ≥44px. Do not rely on color alone to mark status.
